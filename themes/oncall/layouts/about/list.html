About

Hi, I’m Jorge.

Software engineer based in Düsseldorf. I’m a Site Reliability Engineer at trivago, working on observability at scale.

I’m part of the Observability squad at trivago, responsible for designing and operating a logging, metrics and tracing pipeline that moves multiple terabytes of data every day. My job is to make that machinery boring: predictable, well-instrumented, and quiet at 3am.

Before observability I spent a lot of time with search — relevance, query pipelines, and the strange places where data and ranking meet. That curiosity about getting the right answer out of messy data never really left.

Off the clock I build small tools that scratch my own itches: a Touch ID pinentry for GPG, a playground for Filebeat dissect patterns, and a privacy-first analytics platform that runs at the edge.
